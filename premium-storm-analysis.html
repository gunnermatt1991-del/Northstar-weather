Forecast data: Open-Meteo model forecasts. A flagged point is a forecast indicator based on hourly precipitation, wind, pressure, snowfall and weather codes. Wind-force labels use the Beaufort wind-speed thresholds in knots; gusts are shown separately and are not equivalent to sustained wind. Confidence here means agreement between selected model signals only, not a calibrated probability. This is not live radar, an official warning or an emergency alert service.
